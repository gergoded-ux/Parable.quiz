# Eikonia — Quiz curation + site flow (proposal)

_Goal: cut from 126 live to ~80, keep the brand **warm and positive**, lean into the
fun **"Which Bible character are you?"** comparisons, and give the homepage a real
**flow** instead of a flat grid. Reversible — retired quizzes stay in the backlog._

## Curation philosophy

1. **Positive over heavy.** Retire the clinical / wound / divisive clusters for now:
   church-hurt, deconstruction / "angry at God" / dark night, trauma & generational
   wounds, shame / "not enough" / impostor, bitterness, clinical anxiety & burnout,
   grief. They're high-virality but they set a heavy tone and can attract debate. They
   stay in the backlog and can return later as an opt-in **"Deeper / Healing"** section.
2. **Lead with identity + play.** "Which ___ are you?" character quizzes are the most
   shareable, most positive, and most on-brand (a collectible card of *who you are*).
   This is also your hypothesis — the catalog agrees (they cluster at 78-94 virality).
3. **Keep what's genuinely encouraging.** Identity-in-Christ, spiritual gifts/fruit,
   calling, love & relationships, gentle rest, hearing God, forgiveness (the hopeful
   framing), and Bible IQ (fun + neutral).
4. **Order by virality inside each row** (the catalog score), newest/seasonal surfaced
   when relevant (Christmas/Easter).

## The clever indexing: a homepage with an arc

Replace the flat grid with **themed collection rows** in a deliberate emotional order
— playful → "who am I" → relationships → purpose → peace → growth → knowledge. You
land on something fun, get pulled into identity, then go deeper. Each row is a
horizontal carousel ordered by virality; the card art carries it.

```
[ Hero: one rotating featured quiz — "Which Bible Character Are You?" / "Who Are You in Christ?" ]

▸ Which Bible Character Are You?        (the fun comparisons — lead)
▸ Who Am I, Really?                     (identity & worth)
▸ Love & Relationships
▸ Calling & Purpose
▸ Peace & Rest
▸ Walking With God                      (prayer, hearing God, forgiveness)
▸ Test Your Bible Knowledge             (Bible IQ)
```

Nav becomes collection-based and inviting: **Bible Characters · Who Am I · Relationships ·
Faith & Peace · Bible IQ** (friendlier than the current technical "Quizzes / Spiritual
Profiles / Bible IQ"). Each collection also gets its own page (good for SEO: a real
hub-and-spoke structure).
