## 1. Executive summary

Eikonia (eikonia.art) is a free library of beautifully illustrated Christian quizzes that help people see themselves reflected in Scripture and share the result as a collectible card. The name is the Greek word for "image," a nod to the belief that every person bears the image of God.

The product is built to do one thing extremely well: turn a 2-minute moment of self-reflection into a beautiful, shareable artifact. That artifact (a rarity-graded result card paired with a verse) is both the payoff for the user and the engine of growth.

Strategic thesis: there is open white space at the intersection of **shareable + beautiful + faith + zero-friction**. Secular quiz sites are shallow, typology sites are clinical, and faith apps are heavy and account-gated. Eikonia is the warm, no-sign-up, art-first middle that is made to be passed between friends.

Near-term focus: prove the **share loop** (take → get card → share → friend takes) and compound **SEO** across 126+ quizzes, while keeping the experience reverent and clean.

## 2. Product snapshot (what exists today)

- **What it is:** 126 live quizzes (about 260 built, the rest backlog) in three families:
  - **Bible Character** ("Which apostle are you?", archetype matching)
  - **Spiritual Profile** (gift/trait dives, e.g. discernment style, love style)
  - **Bible IQ** (scored knowledge checks)
- **The result:** an illustrated **reward card** with the result name, traits, a verse (American Standard Version, public domain), a match percentage, and a **rarity** (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary).
- **Sharing:** one-tap share to Pinterest, X, Facebook, native share, and copy link, plus a server-rendered Open Graph card image so links unfurl beautifully.
- **Experience:** intro screen, tap-to-advance questions with animation, a "calculating" reveal beat, then the card. No account, no email, scored in the browser.
- **Tech:** Next.js on Vercel, static-fast, privacy-friendly analytics with a full funnel instrumented.
- **Tagline in use:** "What's your reflection?"

## 3. Brand foundation

### Purpose
Make Scripture feel personal, not distant.

### Mission
Help people see something true about themselves, reflected in Scripture, and pass it on.

### Vision
Become the most-shared place on the internet for faith-rooted self-reflection.

### Positioning statement
For curious Christians and the spiritually open who enjoy self-discovery, **Eikonia** is a free, beautifully illustrated quiz experience that mirrors who you are in the language of Scripture and hands you a shareable, collectible card. Unlike generic quiz sites (shallow, secular) or clinical typology platforms (cold, paywalled), Eikonia is warm, reverent, and built to be shared, and unlike most faith apps, it never asks you to sign up or sit through a sermon.

### Brand promise
You will leave with something true, something beautiful, and something to share, in two minutes, for free.

### Name and meaning
**Eikonia** comes from the Greek *eikon* ("image"), the root of "icon," and points to *imago Dei*, the idea that every person carries the image of God (Genesis 1:27). It is intentionally "less obvious" than an overtly churchy name so the brand reads as warm and intriguing to insiders and outsiders alike. Domain: **eikonia.art** (the `.art` reinforces the image/illustration core).

### Brand pillars
1. **Reflective, not preachy.** We hold up a mirror; we do not lecture. Results are for reflection, never a diagnosis or a verdict on someone's faith.
2. **Beautiful by default.** The art is the product. Every card should be worth screenshotting.
3. **Effortless and open.** Free, no sign-up, works on any phone, welcoming to skeptics and saints.
4. **Made to be shared.** Every result is a small gift you give a friend.

### Brand archetype
Primary **Sage** (insight, truth, self-knowledge), blended with **Creator** (the illustrated cards) and a warm note of **Innocent** (hope, gentleness). Explicitly not the Ruler or Hero voice (authoritative, preachy). We are the wise, kind friend, not the pulpit.

### Voice and tone
Warm, literate, gentle, hopeful, lightly poetic. Talks **with** you, not at you. Confident but never smug. Spiritual without jargon.

- **Do:** "See yourself in a new light." "Take what helps, leave the rest." "You keep believing when everything says quit."
- **Do not:** churchy clichés ("do life together," "unpack the Word"), guilt or fear framing, hype-bro marketing ("INSANE results"), clinical jargon ("psychometric instrument"), or flippancy that cheapens faith.
- **Punctuation/style:** plain language, short sentences, no em dashes, sentence case in UI. Scripture quoted from the ASV with reference shown.

## 4. Audience

### Market and category
Two adjacent markets: the **online quiz / self-discovery** space (BuzzFeed, 16Personalities, IDRlabs, Truity) and the **faith / Christian content** space (YouVersion, Hallow, Glorify). Eikonia sits in the gap between them. **[ASSUMPTION]** primary audience is global English-speaking, mobile-first, skewing female 18 to 35 for the core sharer, with a broad long tail.

### Primary personas
1. **Reflective Renee (28, the core sharer).** Christian, on Instagram and Pinterest, loves aesthetic self-discovery content, follows faith creators. Wants: to feel known, and pretty content to share. She is the engine of the viral loop.
2. **Searching Sam (24, the spiritually open).** Curious, maybe deconstructing or church-hurt, wary of anything that feels preachy or salesy. The "less obvious" brand and the no-sign-up, no-sermon promise are what let him in.
3. **Small-Group Sarah (35, the multiplier).** Women's ministry leader, youth pastor, or moms-group host. Uses quizzes as warm, low-pressure icebreakers. One Sarah brings a whole group.
4. **Drop-in Dana (the shared-link visitor).** Arrives from a friend's shared card with zero context. Must be hooked in 5 seconds by the card and a one-tap "find yours."

### Jobs to be done
- "Help me understand myself" (identity, comfort, language for my season).
- "Give me something beautiful and meaningful to post."
- "Give me a fun, safe way to connect my group / friends around faith."
- "Let me kill 2 minutes in a way that feels good, not junky."

## 5. Competitive landscape and differentiation

| Player | Strength | Gap Eikonia exploits |
|---|---|---|
| BuzzFeed / generic quizzes | Viral, shareable, fun | Shallow, secular, ad-cluttered, disposable |
| 16Personalities / Truity / IDRlabs | Credible typology, depth | Cold, text-heavy, no faith lens, depth paywalled |
| YouVersion / Hallow / Glorify / Pray.com | Deep, trusted faith brands | Heavy, account-required, devotional not playful, not quiz/share native |
| Scattered Christian quizzes (Pinterest/blogs) | Exist, some demand | No brand, low quality, no card system, no breadth |

**Eikonia's defensible differentiators:**
- **The reward card + rarity system.** A collectible, gamified, screenshot-worthy artifact that no faith competitor has. Rarity (Common to Legendary) adds surprise, status, and a reason to retake and to compare.
- **Art-first.** Original illustrated covers and cards in a consistent storybook style.
- **Zero friction.** No account, no email, instant result, works everywhere.
- **Breadth.** 126+ quizzes across three formats, compounding for SEO and repeat visits.
- **Inclusive, "less obvious" brand.** Welcomes the curious, not just the committed.
- **Scripture pairing** in the public-domain ASV, so every result feels rooted.

## 7. Visual identity

### Wordmark
"Eikonia" set in an extra-bold, tight-tracked sans (Inter), warm brown. Keep it simple and confident. The word is the logo for now.

### Color palette (live design tokens)
- Backgrounds: cream-1 `#fdf5ee`, cream-2 `#f6e7d8`, sand `#f0dcc4`
- Accents: rose `#e8c9a7`, rose-dark `#d4a574`
- Brand ink: brown `#6b4423`, brown-dark `#4a2f15`
- Text: ink `#2d2a26`, ink-soft `#4a3c2e`, ink-mute `#8a6a47`
- Rarity: Common green `#3d9b4a`, Rare sapphire `#2f7fc0`, Epic purple `#7c3aa0`, Legendary gold `#b8860b`

The palette is warm, golden-hour, reverent. Avoid cold blues, neon, or high-contrast "tech" looks outside the rarity accents.

### Typography
- **Display:** Cinzel (engraved, classical, used on cards and headings)
- **Body/UI:** Inter
- **Scripture / quotes:** EB Garamond italic

### Illustration and reward-card system
House art style (use verbatim for every commissioned image): soft 2D animated-storybook illustration, painterly cel shading, warm golden-hour biblical and ancient-Near-East palette (cream, sand, rose, gold, soft teal), gentle diffused light, reverent and hopeful, one clear central subject, generous breathing room, no text in the image. **Never show the face of God or Jesus** (distant, silhouetted, or from behind only). Six art types: character, creature, object, place, scene, symbol.

### Rarity as a brand mechanic
Rarity is not just a label, it is a core brand experience: the stained-glass frame, the star count, and the gold "Legendary" moment are what make a card worth sharing and collecting. Protect this. Do not dilute it with too many tiers or by making Legendary trivial to get.

### Share / OG cards
Every result renders a 1080x1350 card and an Open Graph image so shared links unfurl as art, not a blue link. This is a primary acquisition surface, treat it as an ad unit you give away.

### Do / Do not
- **Do:** keep layouts airy, let the art breathe, use the warm palette, quote Scripture in EB Garamond.
- **Do not:** show the face of God or Jesus, crowd the cards with text, use stocky or photorealistic imagery, or mix in cold/tech palettes.

## 8. Growth strategy

### North Star metric
**Shares per 100 quiz completions** (proxy for the viral coefficient). Sharing is the engine, so this is what we optimize. Secondary headline metric: **weekly quizzes completed.**

### KPI tree (mapped to the analytics already instrumented)
- **Acquisition:** sessions; traffic by source; `result_view` with `entry = external/direct` (inbound from shared links).
- **Activation:** `quiz_start` to `quiz_complete` rate (completion rate); `quiz_abandon` grouped by `question` (find and fix leaks).
- **Engagement:** quizzes per session; `quiz_card_click` and `related_quiz_click` (discovery); take-another rate.
- **Virality:** `share_click` per `quiz_complete` (share rate); inbound external visits per share (loop efficiency, K-factor).
- **Monetization (later):** ad impressions, RPM, revenue per session.

### Growth loops
1. **Viral share loop (primary).** Discover a quiz, take it, get a rarity card worth showing off, share it, a friend sees it and takes it. Optimize each step: card quality (screenshot-worthy), share friction (one tap), and the landing hook for drop-in visitors. Measure with `share_click` and external `result_view`.
2. **SEO long-tail loop (compounding).** 126+ quizzes, each with a quiz page and many result pages, target "which [figure] are you," "spiritual gifts test free," "[topic] bible quiz." FAQ and Quiz structured data, the /quizzes directory, and rich OG cards lift click-through. Content compounds over time at near-zero marginal cost.
3. **Seasonal loop.** Ride the calendar (Advent, Christmas, Lent, Easter, New Year, Mother's and Father's Day) with timely quizzes and pushes.

### Funnel and lifecycle
Acquisition (social/SEO) → Activation (first quiz completed) → Sharing (card posted) → Retention (take another, return for new/seasonal quizzes) → Monetization (ads, later premium). Email capture is a deliberate later step so we never break the no-sign-up promise on the core flow.

## 9. Channel strategy

Prioritized for a bootstrapped, organic-first start.

1. **Pinterest (priority 1).** The reward cards are natively pinnable and the audience lives here. Create boards by theme (identity, anxiety, Bible characters, Advent). Pin vertical result cards linking to the quiz. Pinterest doubles as a search engine for this demographic and has a long content half-life.
2. **SEO (priority 2, compounding).** Optimize quiz titles and meta, strengthen internal linking (related quizzes, the directory), keep the sitemap and schema healthy, and target long-tail "which X are you" and topical faith queries. This is the durable moat.
3. **Instagram / TikTok / Reels.** Short card-reveal videos ("POV: you got Legendary"), "which apostle are you" trends, carousel breakdowns. Link in bio to the directory.
4. **Facebook groups.** Christian women's, moms', and small-group communities share at high rates; the built-in FB share button feeds this. Participate value-first, never spammy.
5. **Reddit and forums.** Selective, value-first posts in relevant communities; expect strict self-promo rules.
6. **Creators / influencers.** Partner with mid-size Christian creators for quiz takeovers or co-branded quizzes; their audience is pre-qualified.
7. **Email / newsletter (later).** Opt-in only, "new quiz of the week" and seasonal. Never gate the core experience.
8. **Referral.** Already inherent in sharing; consider a light "challenge a friend" prompt on the result page.

## 11. Launch and campaign plan

- **Phase 0 (now): Foundation.** Brand, site, 126 quizzes, reward cards, quiz UX, and analytics are live. Connect eikonia.art and the hello@ inbox. Make sure shared links and OG images resolve.
- **Phase 1: Soft launch (weeks 1 to 4).** Seed Pinterest boards, post a handful of IG/TikTok reveals, share in two or three communities. Watch the funnel daily. Fix the biggest `quiz_abandon` leaks and weak cards. Goal: validate the share loop on a small scale.
- **Phase 2: Ramp (months 2 to 4).** Double down on the best-performing channel, run the first seasonal push, begin creator outreach, and turn on ads once traffic justifies the RPM. Goal: repeatable acquisition.
- **Phase 3: Scale (months 4+).** Add opt-in email, expand quizzes, pursue partnerships and B2B2C (church/group tools), and test monetization beyond ads. Goal: durable growth and revenue.

## 12. Monetization

Keep the core free forever. Layer revenue without harming the reflective feel.

- **Now / near-term:** display ads via the existing ad slots, enabled once traffic warrants a real RPM. Cap density (one or two tasteful slots) to protect trust.
- **Mid-term options (to test):**
  - Premium card packs, printables, or high-res downloads of result cards.
  - Church / small-group tools: themed quiz packs, custom or branded quizzes (B2B2C).
  - Affiliate links to Bibles and Christian books relevant to a result.
  - A tip jar / "support Eikonia" donation.
  - Sponsorships from aligned faith brands (clearly labeled).

Guiding rule: revenue must never make the product feel cheaper or pushier.

## 13. Measurement and analytics

- **Events live:** `quiz_card_click`, `related_quiz_click`, `quiz_start`, `quiz_abandon` (question, total), `quiz_complete` (result), `result_view` (entry: internal/external/direct), `share_click` (platform), plus page views and Speed Insights.
- **Core dashboards to build:** funnel (start to complete to share), drop-off histogram by question, share rate by quiz, inbound-from-share trend (loop health), and top quizzes by completion and by share.
- **Proposed first targets [ASSUMPTION, validate]:** completion rate above 60 percent, share rate above 15 percent of completions, and a steadily rising external `result_view` share as the loop compounds.
- **Hygiene:** keep event properties low-cardinality (slug, result key, platform), and UTM-tag all owned posts so referrer data stays clean.

## 14. Risks, guardrails, and brand safety

- **Theological sensitivity.** Quizzes are for reflection, not doctrine. Use the ASV, avoid denominational landmines, keep the "not a diagnosis" disclaimer, and never show the face of God or Jesus.
- **Tone risk.** Avoid both extremes: churchy cringe and irreverent flippancy. The brand lives in warm, literate sincerity.
- **Sensitive topics.** Mental-health-adjacent quizzes (anxiety, burnout, grief) must stay gentle, carry the "not a substitute for care" line, and point toward real help.
- **AI perception.** Be transparent that people research, write, and review every quiz; do not overclaim; protect quality. Trust is the moat.
- **Monetization vs trust.** Under-monetize early. Ads and upsells must never crowd the reflective experience.
- **Trademark / naming.** "Eikonia" is distinct; the finance-world "Eikon" is being retired and is a different class. Monitor, and do a formal check before major spend.
- **Privacy.** Already minimal (no accounts, in-browser scoring). Keep the policy current and honor requests.
